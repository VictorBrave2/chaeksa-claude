[화풍]
Korean webtoon style, semi-realistic anime illustration, cinematic, soft film grain.
Palette: deep indigo and violet dusk, warm amber lamp light, one small red camellia somewhere in the frame. Seoul setting with subtle city bokeh.

[크기와 구도]
Vertical portrait 1024x1536 (2:3).
Characters in the upper half. The bottom 40% fades into dark indigo with no objects or faces (a text box will cover it).
No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.

[두 사람 — 모든 그림에서 같은 얼굴, 같은 옷]
HIM: Korean man, early 30s, short dark tousled hair, calm gentle eyes, quiet reserved expression; white shirt or dark knit, dark coat outside.
HER: Korean woman, late 20s, dark brown hair (long, or tied in a loose bun), bright expressive face, animated gestures; black coat with red scarf outside, cream knit inside.
